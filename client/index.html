Settings

The arena is meant to be dark — the glow is most of the look. Nudge this if your screen is dimmer than mine, not to turn the night off.

Softens the picture instead of dropping frames when the arena gets busy. Never goes above the resolution you picked, and never below half of it.

A locked 30 is steadier than a wandering 45 and kinder to a battery — but 60 is where aiming feels best. Uncapped is the default.

How far the view turns for a given swipe or mouse movement. Applies to both, and takes effect immediately — drag it mid-match until it feels right.

Calls out kills, headshots and level-ups using your device’s own voice. Off by default — it shares a channel with screen readers, and not everyone wants their game talking.

Gently pulls your aim onto a nearby chicken. Auto turns it on for touch and off for a mouse — a thumb needs the help, and a pointer is already exact, so assist just fights it.

Thumb reach varies by hand and phone, so FIRE and JUMP can each be dragged anywhere. Turn this on, drag them in your next match, then turn it off.

Holding FIRE and sliding your thumb keeps shooting while you aim — you never need to lift off to track someone.

Lower resolution and glow off give the biggest speed-up on older phones. Applies to your next match.
